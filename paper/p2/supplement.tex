\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains the complete proofs of the constructions of the main text that are not in Paper~I: the
finite comparator, the explicit planar rearrangement of convex functions and its lift to the fibres, the scalar
comparison datum for the four-dimensional cubic model, the bordered handle construction with its layer maps, finite
coordinate primitive and Moser fields, the diagonal affine quadric model and its transfer to the ball, the two models
for one large ball, the input-name programs, the finite compilation, the trajectory certificates and the termination
of the algorithm. Appendix~\ref{p2:sec-quoted} reproduces the statements of Paper~I used in the proofs.
\end{abstract}
