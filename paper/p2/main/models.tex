\section{The models}\label{p2:sec-models}

Theorem~\ref{thm:mech-packing} reduces the packing problem to three inputs: a toric model with handle data, a
comparison datum for its profile, and a transfer to the target. This section states the packing theorem for each
model of Table~\ref{tab:mech-models}: the cubic model of Paper~I with general capacities, and the three
higher-dimensional models. It then fixes the case distinction that selects them and the embedding datum it produces.
The proofs are in \Ssec{sec:four-general} (dimension four), \Ssec{sec:qdr}, \Ssec{sec:vtr} (dimension $2n\ge6$,
capacities below one half) and \Ssec{sec:lrg}--\Snum{sec:sxr} (one large ball). Throughout, the target is $\Bo(1)$ and
$\rho_i=c_i/C$.

\subsection{Dimension four, general capacities}\label{sec:mdl-four}

Paper~I proves the packing in the cubic model for \emph{valid cubic data} (\PoneRef{def:shd4-data}): auxiliary
capacities $a_i>\rho_i$, a height $h<\frac13$, a number $0<g\le3(1-3h)$, and a comparison function $K$ with a planar
Hamiltonian isotopy, the identity near $\pi|z|^2=h$, satisfying the comparison inequality for the profile
$3(1-3p)-\sum_i(a_i-p)_+$ with gap $\frac{31}{32}g$ (\PoneRef{thm:cub-packing}). It constructs such data with a linear
$K$ for $k\ge10$ equal capacities with $\frac34<\rho\sqrt k<1$. For $k=9$ that construction would need $a=h$, and for
some unequal inputs no linear $K$ exists (\Sref{Remark}{rem:cmp-linear-fails}). The scalar comparison datum covers all
inputs: the scalar iteration of \Ssec{sec:scalar-iteration}, an analogue of Proposition~\ref{prop:cmp-fixed-point}
over real data, with a fixed mollifier, and a planar isotopy
built from quadratic wells, an Alexander path and the planar machine of Paper~I
(\Sref{Propositions}{prop:scalar-cmp-gap}, \Snum{prop:scalar-planar-realisation} and~\Snum{prop:scalar-cubic-data}).

\begin{theorem}[Packing in dimension four]\label{thm:mdl-cub-general}
Let $k\ge1$ and $0<\rho_1,\dots,\rho_k<\frac13$ with $\sum_i\rho_i^2<1$. Then there are explicit numbers
$\rho_i<\rho_i^+<\rho_i^{\rm dom}$ and smooth maps $f_i\colon\Bo(\rho_i^{\rm dom})\to\C^2$ with symplectic derivative,
injective on $B(\rho_i^+)$, such that the sets $f_i(B(\rho_i^+))$ are pairwise disjoint and contained in
$B(1-\mu_{\rm ball})$ for some $\mu_{\rm ball}\in(0,1)$.
\end{theorem}

This is \Sref{Theorem}{thm:cub-packing-general}. The auxiliary capacities are
$a_i=\rho_i+\delta$ with $\delta=\min\bigl(\frac18(\frac13-\max_i\rho_i),\frac{D}{8(\sum_i\rho_i+k)},\frac18\bigr)$,
$D=1-\sum_i\rho_i^2$, and the height $h<\frac13$ is a closed formula in the $a_i$ (\Sref{Lemma}{lem:cub-auxiliary}); the
comparison gap is $g=\frac34d$ with $d=\frac12\min(\bar P,3(1-3h))$, $\bar P$ the mean of the profile.

\subsection{Dimension $2n\ge6$, capacities below one half}\label{sec:mdl-small}

Let $n\ge3$ and $m=n-1$. The base is a compact piece of the diagonal affine curve
\[
 \Sigma=\{(t,y)\in\C^{m+1}:\ y_j^2=t^2-j,\ 1\le j\le m\},\qquad \Sigma_\ell=\Sigma\cap\{|t|\le R_\ell\},
\]
with $\lambda=d^c\log(1+|z|^2)$, $\sigma=d\lambda$ and $R_\ell=2+\sum_jj+\ell$. The model is
$\Sigma_\ell\times\C^m$ with $\Omega_0=\omega_V+d[(1-2|p|)\lambda]$, $|p|=\sum_j\pi|U_j|^2$, so a ball of capacity
$a$ needs $a<\frac12$. The base area $A_\ell$ satisfies $0\le2^m-A_\ell\le2^m(m+2)/(\ell+2)$, and the limiting
profile $2^m(1-2|p|)$ has integral $1/n!$ over $\{|p|\le\frac12\}$, the volume of $\Bo(1)$
(\Sref{Lemmas}{lem:qdr-curve} and~\Snum{lem:qdr-volume}). The truncation loss is kept in the comparison profile.
The transfer is one map into $\Bo(1)$: a radial injection, a horizontal transport, an inverse Moser return and an
affine ball chart (\Sref{Theorem}{thm:qdr-transfer}).

\begin{theorem}[Packing by small balls in dimension $2n\ge6$]\label{thm:mdl-qdr}
Let $n\ge3$ and $0<\rho_1,\dots,\rho_k<\frac12$ with $\sum_i\rho_i^n<1$, and let $a_1,\dots,a_k$ be real numbers with
$\rho_i<a_i<\frac12$ and $\sum_ia_i^n<1$, and put $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=(\rho_i+2a_i)/3$. The comparator construction of
Theorem~\ref{thm:cmp-main} defines, from these data, smooth symplectic embeddings $f_i$ of open neighbourhoods of the
closed balls $B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})\subset\C^n$ into $\Bo(1)$ and a rational $\mu_{\rm ball}>0$, such that the sets
$f_i(B(\rho_i^{\rm dom}))$ are pairwise disjoint and contained in $B(1-\mu_{\rm ball})$.
\end{theorem}

This is \Sref{Theorem}{thm:qdr-packing}. Construction~\ref{cons:ham-branch} fixes the $a_i$ by explicit
slack increments; the further parameters of the model are those of \Sref{Lemma}{lem:qdr-auxiliary}.

\subsection{One large ball}\label{sec:mdl-lrg}

The quadric model accepts only capacities below one half, and the two-ball inequality allows at most one capacity
$A\ge\frac12$; every other capacity is then below $1-A\le\frac12$. Normalise so that the input is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mdl-large-input}
 A\ge\tfrac12,\qquad A^n+\sum_{i=1}^k\rho_i^n<1,\qquad A+\rho_i<1\quad(1\le i\le k).
\end{equation}
\emph{Valid auxiliary data} are $(a;r_1,\dots,r_k)$ with $\max(A,\frac12)<a<1$, $\rho_i<r_i<1-a$ and
$a^n+\sum_ir_i^n<1$; they exist under \eqref{eq:mdl-large-input}, and rational ones exist as well
(\Sref{Lemma}{lem:lrg-auxiliary}). The large ball is placed in the reserved ball
$\Bo(a)$ and the others in the shell $S_a=\{a<\pi|y|^2<1\}$, of volume $(1-a^n)/n!$; the two-ball condition
$A+\rho_i<1$ is what allows $\rho_i<1-a$ with $a>A$.

For $n\ge4$ the second stage is a product mixed model over a finite bordered piece of the affine quadric curve,
with a radial-integral phase lift, and the return to the shell is an explicit slit-shell formula in coupled
coordinates (\Sref{Lemma}{lem:lrg-slit}). For $n=3$ that base would be a conic; instead we use the polynomial cubic
model of \cite{OpenAI2026}, and the large ball is inserted by a polynomial bubble before the final return
(\Ssec{sec:lrg-six}).

\begin{theorem}[One large ball]\label{thm:mdl-lrg}
Let $n\ge3$, let $A,\rho_1,\dots,\rho_k>0$, and let $(a;r)$ be valid auxiliary data for $(A;\rho)$. For $n\ge4$ put
$\rho_0^{\rm dom}=(A+a)/2$ and $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=(3\rho_i+5r_i)/8$ ($i\ge1$). For $n=3$ assume moreover $a\in\Q$, let
$A<r_0<a$ and $\rho_i<r'_i<r_i$, and put $\rho_0^{\rm dom}=r_0$, $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=r'_i$. Then there are smooth symplectic
embeddings $f_i$ of open neighbourhoods of $B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$ ($0\le i\le k$) into $\Bo(1)$ and a number $\mu_{\rm ball}>0$,
defined explicitly from these data, such that the sets $f_i(B(\rho_i^{\rm dom}))$ are pairwise disjoint and lie in
$B(1-\mu_{\rm ball})$. For $n\ge4$, $f_0$ is the inclusion, $f_0(B(\rho_0^{\rm dom}))\subset\Bo(a)$ and
$f_i(B(\rho_i^{\rm dom}))\subset S_a$ for $i\ge1$.
\end{theorem}

For $n\ge4$ this is \Sref{Theorem}{thm:lrg-high}; for $n=3$ it is \Sref{Theorem}{thm:lrg-six}.
Construction~\ref{cons:ham-branch} fixes $(a;r)$, and for $n=3$ also $r'_i$ and $r_0$, by explicit formulas in $\rho$.

\subsection{The case distinction}\label{p2:sec-models-branch}

The following construction is the case distinction of $H[n,k,C,c,s]$; its linear branch is that of
\PoneRef{cons:ham-branch}. For $n=2$ it uses the cubic model, and for $n\ge3$ it selects one of the three
higher-dimensional models.

\begin{construction}[The normalised embeddings]\label{cons:ham-branch}
\uses{thm:cub-packing, thm:qdr-packing, thm:lrg-high, thm:lrg-six, lem:qdr-auxiliary, lem:lrg-auxiliary}
Let $(n,k,1,\rho)$ be admissible. The \emph{normalised embeddings} $f^\natural_i$, enlarged capacities $\rho_i^+$ and domain capacities
$\rho_i^{\rm dom}$ are defined as follows.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item If $n=2$, they are those of Theorem~\ref{thm:mdl-cub-general}: the maps of \PoneRef{thm:cub-packing} for the valid
 cubic data of the linear datum when $k\ge10$, $\rho_i=\rho$ and $\frac34<\rho\sqrt k<1$, with
 $(\rho_i^+,\rho_i^{\rm dom})=(\rho \varrho_3^2,\rho \varrho_4^2)$ of \PoneRef{rem:rtc-recorded}; and for the valid cubic data of
 \Sref{Proposition}{prop:scalar-cubic-data} otherwise, with $(\rho_i^+,\rho_i^{\rm dom})=(\bar\rho_i,\hat\rho_i)$ of
 \Snum{eq:scalar-enlargement}.
\item If $n\ge3$ and $\rho_i<\frac12$ for all $i$, put
 \[
  \upsilon_i=\min\left(\frac{1/2-\rho_i}{2},
             \frac{1-\sum_j\rho_j^n}{2nk}\right),\qquad a_i=\rho_i+\upsilon_i.
 \]
 Use the quadric construction of \Sref{Theorem}{thm:qdr-packing} with these auxiliary capacities.
 Its enlarged closed capacity and open domain capacity are, respectively,
 $\rho_i^+=\rho_i+\upsilon_i/3$ and $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=\rho_i+2\upsilon_i/3$.
\item If $n\ge4$ and $\rho_j\ge\frac12$, the index $j$ is unique, since $\rho_i+\rho_j<1$ for $i\ne j$. Write $A=\rho_j$,
 $r_*=\max(\{0\}\cup\{\rho_i:i\ne j\})$, $V=1-\sum_i\rho_i^n$, and put
 \[
 \upsilon=\min\left(\frac{1-A-r_*}{4},\,1,\,\frac{V}{2nk2^n}\right),\qquad
 a=A+\upsilon,\qquad r_i=\rho_i+\upsilon \quad(i\ne j).
 \]
 Use the construction of \Sref{Theorem}{thm:lrg-high}. For the distinguished ball set
 $(\rho_j^+,\rho_j^{\rm dom})=(A+\upsilon/2,A+\upsilon)$; its map is the inclusion, defined and injective on all of $\C^n$, and
 Theorem~\ref{thm:mdl-lrg} places $B((A+a)/2)=B(\rho_j^+)$ in $\Bo(a)$. For $i\ne j$, set
 $(\rho_i^+,\rho_i^{\rm dom})=((11\rho_i+5r_i)/16,(3\rho_i+5r_i)/8)$.
\item If $n=3$ and $\rho_j\ge\frac12$, put $A=\rho_j$ and
 \[
 \gamma_0=\min\left(1-\sum_i\rho_i^3,\,1-A,\,
                    \min_{i\ne j}(1-A-\rho_i)\right),\qquad
 \eta=\frac{\gamma_0}{100(k+1)}.
 \]
 The minimum over an empty set is omitted. Set
 \[
 M=\lceil1/\eta\rceil+1,\qquad
 a=\frac{\lfloor(A+4\eta)M\rfloor+1}{M}.
 \]
 Use the polynomial construction of \Sref{Theorem}{thm:lrg-six}, with distinguished capacity
 $r_0=A+2\eta$. The auxiliary small family retains all $k$ indices: for $i\ne j$ its outer and
 inner capacities are $r_i=\rho_i+3\eta$ and $r'_i=\rho_i+2\eta$, while the $j$-th auxiliary
 ball has input capacity $\eta/4$ and capacities $r_j=\eta$, $r'_j=\eta/2$, so that the small family is nonempty and
 indexed like the input. The comparison, the weights and the transfer are those of this auxiliary family of $k$ balls,
 including the $j$-th ball in every parameter and compact union. The final $j$-th map is the bubble map of the
 distinguished ball; the map of the auxiliary $j$-th ball is not used.
 Set $(\rho_i^+,\rho_i^{\rm dom})=(\rho_i+\eta,\rho_i+2\eta)$ for every final ball.
\end{enumerate}
For the toric maps in (ii)--(iv), use the finite-atlas normalization and three inverse Moser endpoints of
\Sref{Construction}{cons:native-packing}, applied to the closed source balls $B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$.
In particular the cap smoothing and layer strip use its internal capacity
$(3a_i+\rho_i^{\rm dom})/4$, with $a_i$ replaced by $r_i$ in cases (iii) and (iv); the distinguished ball, placed by
inclusion or by the bubble, is exempt.
\end{construction}

\emph{Verification of the hypotheses.} All increments $\upsilon_i$, $\upsilon$, $\eta$ are positive: admissibility gives
$\sum_i\rho_i^n<1$, $\rho_i<\frac12$ in case (ii), and $A+\rho_i<1$ for $i\ne j$ together with $A<1$ in cases (iii) and (iv). We use
$(x+u)^n-x^n=n\xi^{n-1}u$ for some $\xi\in[x,x+u]$.

\emph{Case (ii).} $a_i\le\rho_i+(\frac12-\rho_i)/2<\frac12$, and, since $0\le\rho_i<a_i<1$,
$\sum_ia_i^n\le\sum_i\rho_i^n+n\sum_i\upsilon_i\le\sum_i\rho_i^n+(1-\sum_j\rho_j^n)/2<1$; these are the hypotheses of
\Sref{Theorem}{thm:qdr-packing}, and $\rho_i^+=(2\rho_i+a_i)/3$, $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=(\rho_i+2a_i)/3$ are the capacities named there.

\emph{Case (iii).} $(a;r)$ is valid auxiliary data (\Sref{Lemma}{lem:lrg-auxiliary}): $a=A+\upsilon >A\ge\frac12$; $a<1$ because
$\upsilon<1-A$; $r_i+a=\rho_i+A+2\upsilon \le r_*+A+\frac12(1-A-r_*)<1$, i.e.\ $r_i<1-a$; and with $0\le x,\upsilon \le1$,
$(x+\upsilon)^n\le x^n+n2^{n-1}\upsilon$, so $a^n+\sum_{i\ne j}r_i^n\le\sum_i\rho_i^n+kn2^n\upsilon\le\sum_i\rho_i^n+V/2<1$. \Sref{Theorem}{thm:lrg-high}
gives $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=(3\rho_i+5r_i)/8$ and $\rho_i<(11\rho_i+5r_i)/16<\rho_i^{\rm dom}<r_i$; for the distinguished ball, the inclusion
is injective on all of $\C^n$ and $B(A+\upsilon/2)\subset\Bo(a)$, which is disjoint from the shell $S_a$ containing the other images.

\emph{Case (iv).} First $1/M<\eta$, so $A+4\eta<a\le A+4\eta+1/M<A+5\eta$, and $a\in\Q$. With $\gamma_0\le1-A$,
$\gamma_0\le1-A-\rho_i$ and $\eta\le\gamma_0/200$: $a<1$; $r_0=A+2\eta\in(A,a)$; $\rho_i<r'_i<r_i$ and
$r_i+a<\rho_i+A+8\eta<1$ for $i\ne j$; and for the auxiliary ball $\eta/4<\eta/2<\eta$ and $\eta+a<A+6\eta<1$. By the mean value
bound with $n=3$ and $x,u\le1$, $a^3\le A^3+60\eta$, $r_i^3\le\rho_i^3+36\eta$ and $\eta^3\le\eta$, so
$a^3+\sum_{i\ne j}r_i^3+\eta^3\le\sum_i\rho_i^3+(36k+25)\eta<\sum_i\rho_i^3+\gamma_0\le1$. Thus $(a;r)$, with the auxiliary $j$-th
entry, is valid auxiliary data with $a$ rational, and \Sref{Theorem}{thm:lrg-six} applies with $r_0$ and the $r'_i$. Its
domains are $B(r_0)=B(\rho_j^{\rm dom})$ and $B(r'_i)=B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$.

The data may have real coefficients, which is allowed at level L1. The polyhedral construction applies over real
coefficients by the observation following Proposition~\ref{prop:cmp-fixed-point}; the finite-output algorithm certifies
its polynomial directly.

\begin{proposition}[The embeddings]\label{prop:ham-embeddings}
\uses{cons:ham-branch, thm:cub-packing-general, thm:qdr-packing, thm:lrg-high, thm:lrg-six}
Let $(n,k,C,c)$ be admissible, $\rho=c/C$, and let $f^\natural_i$, $\rho_i^+$ and $\rho_i^{\rm dom}$ be given by
Construction~\ref{cons:ham-branch}. Put
\[
 c'_i=C\rho_i^+,\qquad \bar c_i=C\rho_i^{\rm dom},\qquad f_i(u)=\sqrt C\,f^\natural_i(u/\sqrt C).
\]
Then $(c'_i,\bar c_i,f_i)$ is an embedding datum for $(n,k,C,c)$, and $\bigcup_if_i(B(c'_i))$ is a compact subset of
$\Bo(C)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In each case $\rho_i<\rho_i^+<\rho_i^{\rm dom}$, and the model theorem gives exactly the properties required on these
balls. In case (i), Theorem~\ref{thm:mdl-cub-general} (\Sref{Theorem}{thm:cub-packing-general}) states them for the
displayed pairs. In cases (ii)--(iv) the model
theorems (\Sref{Theorems}{thm:qdr-packing}, \Snum{thm:lrg-high} and~\Snum{thm:lrg-six}) give smooth symplectic embeddings
of open neighbourhoods of $B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$ with pairwise disjoint images of $B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$ in $B(1-\mu_{\rm ball})$: in
case (ii) $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=(\rho_i+2a_i)/3$; in case (iii) $\rho_i^{\rm dom}=(3\rho_i+5r_i)/8$ for $i\ne j$, while the
distinguished ball is placed by the inclusion, which is symplectic and injective on all of $\C^n$ and maps
$B(\rho_j^+)=B((A+a)/2)$ into $\Bo(a)$, disjoint from the shell $S_a$ containing the other images; in case (iv)
$\rho_i^{\rm dom}=r'_i$ for $i\ne j$ and $\rho_j^{\rm dom}=r_0$, with $r'_i$ and $r_0$ of Construction~\ref{cons:ham-branch}. Since $B(\rho_i^+)\subset\Bo(\rho_i^{\rm dom})\subset B(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$,
this is smoothness and the symplectic identity on $\Bo(\rho_i^{\rm dom})$ and injectivity, pairwise disjointness and
target containment on $B(\rho_i^+)$. Physical rescaling preserves these properties, because
$Df_i(u)=Df_i^\natural(u/\sqrt C)$. The union is a finite union of continuous images of compact sets.
\end{proof}


\begin{definition}[The Hamiltonian $H{[n,k,C,c,s]}$]\label{def:ham-H-final}
\uses{cons:ham-branch, prop:ham-embeddings, S-cons:iso-isotopy, def:ham-H}
On admissible input with pairwise disjoint closed source balls, let $\mathfrak E$ be the embedding datum of
Proposition~\ref{prop:ham-embeddings}, together with the centres $s_i$, and put
$H[n,k,C,c,s]=H_{\mathfrak E}$ (Definition~\ref{I:def:ham-H}).
\end{definition}

In the linear branch of case (i) this is the Hamiltonian of \PoneRef{def:ham-H-final}.
