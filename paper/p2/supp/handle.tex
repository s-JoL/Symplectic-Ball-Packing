\section{The bordered handle construction}\label{p2:sec-shd}

This section gives the handle construction for the higher-dimensional model inputs
(\Mref{Definition}{def:mech-model}) and proves the packing theorem in a model. It is an explicit version of the
surface packing lemma of \cite[\S3.2]{OpenAI2026}. Beyond the general construction, it supplies the finite primitive, the handle data of the
diagonal quadric, and the nondegeneracy and flow statements; the bump forms, tree primitives, normalisation, Moser
fields and the maps $\psi_i$ are fixed in \S\ref{sec:native-toric}, the layer maps in \S\ref{p2:sec-str}. The periodic
four-dimensional instance is \PoneRef{sec:shd-four}.

\subsection{The handle construction}\label{p2:sec-machine-hdl}

We give the handle construction for $n\ge3$, $m=n-1$, in the form used by \Mref{Theorem}{thm:mech-packing}; it
follows the outline of \PoneRef{sec:hdl}, whose twist and suspension lemma are recalled below. Write $\Omega=\omega_V+d\Gamma_{\rm phys}$ on the explicit affine product, with
compact model region $V_\Delta(E)=\Sigma_\ell\times V_\Delta$, physical base curvature $\kappa(p)$ and profile
$\Hb(p)=\int_{\Sigma_\ell}\kappa(p)$. The comparison datum has gap $\gamma$ and reserve $0<\gamma_*\le\gamma$. A horizontal
one-form annihilates fibre tangent vectors; a family of base one-forms $\alpha(p)$ depending smoothly on $p$ gives a
toric horizontal form, with differential $d_\Sigma\alpha(p)+\sum_jdp_j\wedge\partial_j\alpha(p)$.

\emph{The scalar budget.}\label{sec:mech-volume} For a small-ball input, with $\mathcal B_\Delta$ as in (\Mnum{I:eq:mech-model-volume}), the cap identity gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mech-budget}
 \frac{1-\sum_i\rho_i^n}{n!}
 =\left(\frac1{n!}-\mathcal B_\Delta\right)
   +\int_\Delta\Pb
   +\sum_i\frac{a_i^n-\rho_i^n}{n!}.
\end{equation}
The model parameters make the sum of the first and last terms on the right smaller than the left side, so $\int_\Delta\Pb>0$. In the quadric input the reference-area tail controls the difference from the limiting affine profile; the six-dimensional input is in \S\ref{sec:lrg-six}. The comparison uses a minorant with positive mean that is positive near the outer faces. Its reserve pays for the background and collar mass, and $a_i-\rho_i$ pays for the strip and the source neighbourhood. For a large-ball input the limiting budget for the remaining balls is $(1-a^n)/n!$, where $a$ is the reserved capacity; the parameters are in \S\ref{sec:lrg}. In all cases the transfer is applied to the already constructed compact family of model images.

\begin{definition}[Handle data]\label{def:hdl-handle-data}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawAnnularHandle,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.quadricRawHandle,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.quadricModelRawPresentation}
Each higher-dimensional model supplies the following data.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item An explicit annular coordinate map on $|s_{\rm a}|<w$, with working band
$|s_{\rm a}|\le b<w$. We use $s=(s_{\rm a}+b)/(2b)$, so the working band is $[0,1]$.
For the diagonal-quadric presentation the model uses $w=1/8$ and $b=1/16$ inside a double cylinder of half-width $1/4$ on which the annular coordinate is defined.
\item A fixed compact support set $\mathcal B\subset\operatorname{int}\Sigma_\ell$.
\item A smooth closed one-form $\beta$, supported in $\mathcal B$, whose annular expression is
$b(v)\,dv$, with $b\ge0$, $b>0$ on $(v_-,v_+)$ and $\int_0^1b(v)\,dv=1$.
\item An explicit reference primitive $\Gamma_{\rm ref}$ with positive curvature
$\Lambda=d_\Sigma\Gamma_{\rm ref}$, a reference-area upper bound $A_{\rm ref}>0$, and a positive
physical density floor $\kappa_-$ relative to $\Lambda$ on the correction region:
$\kappa(p)\ge\kappa_-\Lambda$ for $p\in\Delta$.
\item A finite coordinate-box presentation of that region: explicit chart maps and inverses,
partition weights, unit-mass coordinate bumps, and a rooted tree on the boxes in which every box other than the root has an overlapping parent of smaller index.
Rectangular integration along this tree defines the operator $\mathcal P$ of \S\ref{sec:native-toric}.
\item The product fibre coordinates of the affine model.
\item A collar cutoff $\chi_\partial$, equal to one near the boundary and zero on the inner region
containing the annulus, together with the model's proved tail bound. A second, larger finite atlas
covers the whole closed model region for the Moser flows.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The fixed annulus and dual form are constructed in \S\ref{sec:vtr}; the finite atlas and tree are in
\S\ref{sec:native-toric}. The annular gauge is identically one on $|s_{\rm a}|<b_1=(b+w)/2$.
We write $\mathcal A^+$ for this open band and $\mathcal A$ for the smaller open band
$|s_{\rm a}|<(b+b_1)/2$; both contain the working band $0\le s\le1$ in their interior, and the clock $f(s)=S(s)$ is
constant on collars of the boundary of $\mathcal A$. Put $\eta=df\wedge\beta$ and write
$\Lambda=a_\Lambda(s,v)\,ds\wedge dv$, $a_\Lambda>0$.

\begin{lemma}[Top power; \PoneTag{lem:hdl-top-power}]\label{I:lem:hdl-top-power}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.Forms.pf_splitForm,
OAI.PackingSufficiencySupport.Hamiltonian.toric_horizontalCoupling_isInvertible,
OAI.PackingSufficiencySupport.Hamiltonian.oneCovectorForm_isInvertible}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item In local product coordinates let $\kappa'$ be a base two-form and $a'_1,\dots,a'_m$ base one-forms, with
coefficients depending on the base point and on $p$. Then
$(\omega_V+\kappa'+\sum_jdp_j\wedge a'_j)^n=n\,\omega_V^m\wedge\kappa'$.
\item On $E|_{\mathcal A^+}$, in the explicit product coordinates, let $\sigma_\Sigma=a_\Sigma(s,v)\,ds\wedge dv$ be positive, let $G(s,w)$ be smooth and
independent of $v$, and $e>0$. Then
$\big(\omega_V+e\sigma_\Sigma+d(G\beta)\big)^n=n\,(e a_\Sigma(s,v)+b(v)\,\partial_sG)\,\omega_V^m\wedge ds\wedge dv$.
\end{enumerate}
In particular the forms in (a) are nondegenerate where $\kappa'>0$; when closed, they are
symplectic there. The forms in (b) are symplectic where $\partial_sG\ge0$.
\end{lemma}

\subsubsection{The first path}\label{sec:hdl-first}
Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-eh}
 e_\Lambda=\min\left(\frac{\gamma_*}{32A_{\rm ref}},\frac{\kappa_-}{2}\right)>0,
 \qquad \epsilon_0=\frac{\gamma_*}{16}.
\end{equation}
The model tail estimate and the reference-area bound give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-collar}
 \int_{\Sigma_\ell}\chi_\partial\kappa(p)\le\frac{\gamma_*}{32},\qquad
 e_\Lambda\int_{\Sigma_\ell}\Lambda\le\frac{\gamma_*}{32}\qquad(p\in\Delta).
\end{equation}
Apply the finite primitive operator $\mathcal P$ of \S\ref{sec:native-toric}, a finite sum of rectangular integrals over
the boxes corrected along the tree so that $d\mathcal P\omega=\omega$ for every two-form $\omega$ of total mass zero, to
$(1-\chi_\partial)(\kappa(p)-e_\Lambda\Lambda)$, then subtract its annular period and an exact annular gauge.
The resulting primitive $\Gamma_n$ is the formula in Construction~\ref{cons:native-normalization}. It has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-ksm}
\begin{gathered}
 \kappa_{\rm sm}(p)=\chi_\partial\kappa(p)+(1-\chi_\partial)e_\Lambda\Lambda,\qquad
 \epsilon_{\rm sm}(p)=\int_{\Sigma_\ell}\kappa_{\rm sm}(p),\\
 H^*(p)=\Hb(p)-\epsilon_{\rm sm}(p),\qquad
 d_\Sigma\Gamma_n(p)=\kappa_{\rm sm}(p)+H^*(p)\eta .
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The mass equality is proved by calibration with the root bump (Lemma~\ref{lem:native-calibration}). Since
$H^*(p)=\int_{\Sigma_\ell}(1-\chi_\partial)(\kappa(p)-e_\Lambda\Lambda)$ and $\kappa(p)\ge\kappa_-\Lambda\ge2e_\Lambda\Lambda$ on the
correction region, $H^*\ge0$ on $\Delta$; and by \eqref{eq:hdl-collar}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-budget}
 0\le\epsilon_{\rm sm}\le\epsilon_0\le\gamma/16,\qquad
 H^*\ge\Hb-\gamma_*/16.
\end{equation}
On the annulus the period and gauge corrections give
$\Gamma_n=e_\Lambda\Gamma_{\rm ref}+f(s)H^*(p)\beta$ on an open neighbourhood of the working band. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-alpha1}
 \alpha_1=\Gamma_n-\Gamma_{\rm phys}.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[First path]\label{prop:hdl-first-path}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.normalization_target,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.relativePath_semantic,
SymplecticPacking.Bundle.alpha1_curvature, SymplecticPacking.Bundle.Cubic.alpha1_annulus}
The form $\Omega_\lambda=\Omega+\lambda d\alpha_1$, $0\le\lambda\le1$, is symplectic on a
neighbourhood of the model region. Its base curvature is the positive convex combination of
$\kappa$ and $d_\Sigma\Gamma_n$, and on the annulus its endpoint is
\[
 \Omega_1=\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d\big(f(s)H^*(p)\beta\big).
\]
The variation primitive is horizontal and toric, is smooth in the moment variables, and vanishes
near the outer base boundary.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The base curvature and annular normal form follow from the normalisation identities. Toric mixed terms do not affect the top power (Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-top-power}(a)), so the positive convex combination is nondegenerate. The difference $\Gamma_n-\Gamma_{\rm phys}$ is compactly supported in the interior of the base. Smoothness in the moment variables follows from the explicit primitive formulas.
\end{proof}


\subsubsection{The twist}\label{I:sec:hdl-twist}
On a collar of each outer face the generators $Q_t$ of the comparator Poisson-commute with $\mu_\nu$, so
$q_t(V_\Delta)=V_\Delta$, $q_t(\operatorname{int}V_\Delta)=\operatorname{int}V_\Delta$, and $q_t$ maps each outer face into itself.
We write $q=q_1$. For $\lambda\in[0,1]$ define on $E|_{\mathcal A}$
\begin{equation}\label{I:eq:hdl-twist}
 \mathcal D_\lambda(s,v,w)=\big(s,v,q_{\lambda f(s)}(w)\big),\qquad
 Q^\lambda_s=\lambda f'(s)\,Q_{\lambda f(s)},\qquad
 G_\lambda(s,w)=f(s)\,H^*\big(p(q_\lambda^{-1}(w))\big),
\end{equation}
and $\mathcal D=\mathcal D_1$; then $\partial_sq_{\lambda f(s)}=X_{Q^\lambda_s}\circ q_{\lambda f(s)}$.

\begin{lemma}[Suspension; \PoneTag{lem:hdl-suspension}]\label{I:lem:hdl-suspension}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.firstPath_semantic}
On $E|_{\mathcal A}$, for $\lambda\in[0,1]$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $\mathcal D_\lambda^*\omega_V=\omega_V+d\big[(Q^\lambda_s\circ q_{\lambda f(s)})\,ds\big]$;
\item with the horizontal one-form
\[
 \eta_\lambda=(Q^\lambda_s\circ q_{\lambda f(s)})\,ds
 +f(s)\Big[H^*\big(p(q_\lambda^{-1}q_{\lambda f(s)}(w))\big)-H^*\big(p(w)\big)\Big]\beta ,
\]
one has
$\mathcal D_\lambda^*\big(\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(G_\lambda\beta)\big)=\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d\big(f(s)H^*(p)\beta\big)+d\eta_\lambda$;
\item $\eta_0=0$, $\eta_\lambda$ is smooth in $(\lambda,s,v,w)$, and $\eta_\lambda=0$ for $s\notin(0,1)$.
Hence $\eta_\lambda$ extends by zero to a horizontal one-form on a neighbourhood of $V_\Delta(E)$, supported over
$\mathcal A$, and $\Omega_1+d\eta_\lambda=\mathcal D_\lambda^*\big(\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(G_\lambda\beta)\big)$ on $E|_{\mathcal A}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

The second path is $\Omega^{(1)}_\lambda=\Omega_1+d\eta_\lambda$; by Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-top-power}(b) and
Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-nbhd} it is an exact symplectic path on a neighbourhood of $V_\Delta(E)$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:shd-second-path}). The map $\mathcal D_\lambda$ itself is defined only over $\mathcal A$; only
the forms are glued to $E$.

\subsubsection{Layers and the Moser map}\label{sec:hdl-layers}
Fix source capacities $\rho_i^\sharp<a_i$ and put $\sigma_i=(a_i-\rho_i^\sharp)/2>0$.
Define the smooth caps and the collar coefficient by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-caps}
 h_i(p)=(a_i-|p|)S\big((a_i-|p|)/\sigma_i\big)\quad(1\le i\le k),\qquad
 J=K-H^*=K-\Hb+\epsilon_{\rm sm} .
\end{equation}
Put $N=k+1$ and $D_s=1/(3N+1)$. For $j=1,\dots,N$ set
$l_j=(3j-2)D_s$, $r_j=(3j-1)D_s$, $I_j=[l_j,r_j]$ and
$\varsigma_j(s)=S\big(2(s-l_j-D_s/4)/D_s\big)$; the last index $j=N$ is denoted by $*$.
The intervals are ordered and disjoint in $(0,1)$, and each clock is constant on collars
of its interval endpoints, with $\varsigma_j'\ge0$.

\begin{proposition}[Layers]\label{prop:hdl-layers}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.smoothCap_smooth,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.smoothCap_nonneg,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.smoothCap_le,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.smoothCap_ge}
Under the hypotheses of \Mref{Theorem}{thm:mech-packing}, with $\epsilon_{\rm sm}$ from \eqref{eq:hdl-ksm} and $h_i,J$ from \eqref{eq:hdl-caps}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item each $h_i$ is smooth on $\R^m$, and
$0\le h_i(p)\le(a_i-|p|)_+$ and $(a_i+\rho_i^\sharp)/2-|p|\le h_i(p)$;
it vanishes for $|p|\ge a_i$ and equals $a_i-|p|$ for $|p|\le(a_i+\rho_i^\sharp)/2$;
\item the residual $R^*(w)=K\big(p(q^{-1}w)\big)-J\big(p(w)\big)-\sum_{i=1}^kh_i\big(p(w)\big)$ satisfies, for $w\in V_\Delta$,
\[
 R^*(w)\ \ge\ \gamma-\sup_\Delta\epsilon_{\rm sm}\ \ge\ \frac{15\gamma}{16}>0 ;
\]
\item the final coefficient
$G_{\rm f}(s,w)=J(p(w))+\sum_{i=1}^k\varsigma_i(s)h_i(p(w))+\varsigma_*(s)R^*(w)$ satisfies  $\partial_sG_{\rm f}\ge0$ on
$\mathcal A\times V_\Delta$; $G_{\rm f}=J\circ p$ for $s\le l_1$; $G_{\rm f}=K\circ p\circ q^{-1}$ for
$s\ge r_*$; and on $I_i\times V_\Delta$, $1\le i\le k$,
\[
 G_{\rm f}(s,w)=A_i(p(w))+\varsigma_i(s)h_i(p(w)),\qquad A_i=J+\sum_{j<i}h_j .
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) Write $x=a_i-|p|$, so $h_i=x\,S(x/\sigma_i)$ is a smooth function of $p$, and $(a_i+\rho_i^\sharp)/2-|p|=x-\sigma_i$. Recall
$0\le S\le1$, $S=0$ on $(-\infty,0]$ and $S=1$ on $[1,\infty)$. If $x\le0$, then $h_i=0=x_+$ and $x-\sigma_i<0$. If
$0<x<\sigma_i$, then $0\le h_i\le x=x_+$ and $x-\sigma_i<0\le h_i$. If $x\ge\sigma_i$, then $h_i=x=x_+$. This gives all
claims of (i).

(ii) For $w\in V_\Delta$, put $z=q^{-1}(w)$. By $J=K-\Hb+\epsilon_{\rm sm}$, the hypothesis $\Hb-\sum_i(a_i-|p|)_+\ge P$ and (i), $R^*(w)\ge K(p(z))-(K-P)(p(qz))-\sup_\Delta\epsilon_{\rm sm}\ge\gamma-\sup_\Delta\epsilon_{\rm sm}$ by \Mref{Definition}{def:mech-datum} at $z$. Finally $\epsilon_{\rm sm}\le\gamma/16$ by \eqref{eq:hdl-budget}.

(iii) $\partial_sG_{\rm f}=\sum_i\varsigma_i'h_i+\varsigma_*'R^*\ge0$ by (i) and (ii). For $s\le l_1$ all clocks vanish; for $s\ge r_*$ all equal one, and $J+\sum_ih_i+R^*=K\circ p\circ q^{-1}$. On $I_i$, $\varsigma_j=1$ for $j<i$ and $\varsigma_j=0$ for $j>i$, including $j=*$.
\end{proof}


\emph{The third path.} Keep $\mathcal D$ fixed and interpolate the coefficient from $G_1=f(s)H^*\circ p\circ q^{-1}$ to
$G_{\rm f}$. On $E|_{\mathcal A}$ put $\xi_{\mathcal A}=((G_{\rm f}-G_1)\circ\mathcal D)\,\beta$; it equals $J(p)\beta$ on both
collars of $\mathcal A$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-xi}
 \xi=J(p)\,\mathrm{pr}^*\beta+\operatorname{ext}_0\big(\xi_{\mathcal A}-J(p)\beta\big)
\end{equation}
is a horizontal one-form on a neighbourhood of $V_\Delta(E)$. Put $\Omega^{(2)}_\tau=\Omega^{(1)}_1+\tau\,d\xi$ and
$\Omega_{\rm final}=\Omega^{(2)}_1$. On $E|_{\mathcal A}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-final}
\begin{aligned}
 \Omega^{(2)}_\tau&=\mathcal D^*\big(\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(\hat G_\tau\beta)\big),&\hat G_\tau&=(1-\tau)G_1+\tau G_{\rm f},\\
 \Omega_{\rm final}&=\mathcal D^*\Omega_{\rm pre},&\Omega_{\rm pre}&=\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(G_{\rm f}\beta);
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
off $E|_{\mathcal A}$, $\Omega^{(2)}_\tau=\Omega_1+\tau\,dJ\wedge\beta$ differs from $\Omega_1$ by a toric mixed term. Every
$\Omega^{(2)}_\tau$ is symplectic on a neighbourhood of $V_\Delta(E)$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:shd-layer-path}).

For each of the three paths write its primitive as $\lambda_{j,t}=\lambda_V+Q_{j,t}$ and its variation
as $\nu_{j,t}=\partial_tQ_{j,t}$; these are $\alpha_1$, $\partial_t\eta_t$ and $\xi$, respectively.
In the larger finite atlas use the whole-path Gram cutoff $\chi_j$ of
Construction~\ref{cons:native-packing}. Define the cut field by
$\iota_{Y_{j,t}}d\lambda_{j,t}=-\chi_j\nu_{j,t}$, computed in the charts of the finite atlas, and let $\Phi_j$ be
its time-one map. Put $\Phi=\Phi_2\circ\Phi_1\circ\Phi_0$ and $\Phi^{-1}=\Phi_0^{-1}\circ\Phi_1^{-1}\circ\Phi_2^{-1}$.

\begin{proposition}[Moser maps]\label{prop:hdl-faces}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawFlowAtlasSpec.pathFlow_eq_horizontalMoserMap,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation.fullMoserFlow_diffeomorph,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.native_packing_spec}
The three cut fields are smooth with common compact spatial support. Their time-one maps are
diffeomorphisms, preserve the model region and its interior in both directions, and satisfy
$\Phi_j^*\Omega_{j,1}=\Omega_{j,0}$ there. Consequently $\Phi^*\Omega_{\rm final}=\Omega$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Faces.} Near each outer face, every path form is invariant under the flow of the face moment $\mu_\nu$: the toric terms are invariant, and the comparator isotopy commutes with this flow because $Q_t$ Poisson-commutes with $\mu_\nu$ on the collar (\Mref{Definition}{def:mech-datum}\ref*{M-it:mech-datum-collar}). Hence $\iota_{X_{\mu_\nu}}\Omega_{j,t}=-d\mu_\nu$ there. The variation primitives annihilate $X_{\mu_\nu}$, so $d\mu_\nu(Y_{j,t})=0$ near the boundary value, and no trajectory crosses a face level, or an intersection of faces, in either direction.

\emph{Completeness.} The variation primitives vanish near the outer base boundary and the fields have compact support, so the flows are complete, with the backward flows as inverses.

\emph{Pullback.} On the model region $\chi_j=1$, and Cartan's formula gives $\frac d{dt}\Phi_{j,t}^*\Omega_{j,t}=\Phi_{j,t}^*d(\iota_{Y_{j,t}}\Omega_{j,t}+\nu_{j,t})=0$; the resulting coordinate ODEs and inverse endpoints are the maps of the construction.
\end{proof}

\emph{Layer coordinates.} On $\Lambda_i=\{l_i<s<r_i,\ v_-<v<v_+\}\times\operatorname{int}V_\Delta$ let $T(v)=\int_{v_-}^vb$, a diffeomorphism $(v_-,v_+)\to(0,1)$ with $dT=\beta$. Define

\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-layer-x}
 \ell(s,T)=\frac{e_\Lambda a_\Lambda(s,v(T))}{b(v(T))},\qquad
 x_i(s,T,p)=\int_{l_i}^s\ell(u,T)\,du+\varsigma_i(s)h_i(p).
\end{equation}

Then $\partial_sx_i=\ell+\varsigma_i'h_i>0$, $x_i=0$ at $s=l_i$, and $x_i>h_i(p)$ at $s=r_i$, so for $0<x<h_i(p)$ and $0<T<1$ there is a unique root $s=s_i(x,T,p)\in(l_i,r_i)$, smooth by the implicit function theorem. Since $e_\Lambda\Lambda=\ell\,ds\wedge dT$ and, by Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-layers}(iii), $d(G_{\rm f}\beta)=(\varsigma_i'h_i\,ds+dA_i+\varsigma_i\,dh_i)\wedge dT$ on $\Lambda_i$,

\begin{equation}\label{eq:hdl-layer-form}
 \Omega_{\rm pre}=\omega_V+dx_i\wedge dT+dA_i(p)\wedge dT\qquad\text{on }\Lambda_i .
\end{equation}

The multi-angle shear $\mathrm{Sh}_A$ (Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-shear}) removes $dA_i(p)\wedge dT$, and the disc-to-strip map $\Psi_\delta$ (Proposition~\ref{I:prop:hdl-strip}) supplies $(x,T)$.



\subsection{The finite primitive}\label{sec:shd-primitive}

\begin{construction}[Coordinate primitive]\label{cons:shd-primitive}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawAmbientBox.formPrimitive,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation.primitiveOperator}
Let $r\,dx\wedge dy$ be a smooth two-form with compact support in an open coordinate rectangle $I\times I'$, put
$M(x)=\int r(x,y)\,dy$ and fix a smooth bump $b$ with compact support in $I'$ and $\int b=1$. With integrals starting
at the lower endpoints of $I$ and $I'$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shd-Ploc}
 P(r)=-\left[\int^y\bigl(r(x,t)-b(t)M(x)\bigr)\,dt\right]dx
       +b(y)\left[\int^xM(t)\,dt\right]dy .
\end{equation}
For a finite presentation (Definition~\ref{def:shd-presentation}) with partition weights $\zeta_i$, unit-mass bumps
$\eta_i$ and tree primitives $T_i$ (\S\ref{sec:native-toric}), the operator is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shd-PSigma}
 \mathcal P\omega=\sum_i\left[P_i(\zeta_i\omega-m_i(\omega)\eta_i)+m_i(\omega)T_i\right],\qquad m_i(\omega)=\int\zeta_i\omega .
\end{equation}
Each local coefficient is extended by zero only after multiplication by its compactly supported weight.
\end{construction}

\begin{lemma}[Primitive identity]\label{lem:shd-primitive}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation.primitiveOperator_data,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation.primitiveOperator_family_smooth}
If $\int_{I\times I'}r\,dx\wedge dy=0$, then $P(r)$ has compact support in $I\times I'$ and $dP(r)=r\,dx\wedge dy$. Let
$\omega(p)$ be a smooth family of two-forms supported in the correction core with $m(\omega(p))=\sum_im_i(\omega(p))=0$.
Then $d_\Sigma\mathcal P\omega(p)=\omega(p)$, and $\mathcal P\omega(p)$ has fixed compact support in the interior of the
model base and depends smoothly on $(p,x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The differential of the first term of \eqref{eq:shd-Ploc} is $(r-bM)\,dx\wedge dy$, that of the second is
$bM\,dx\wedge dy$. The $dx$-coefficient vanishes for $y$ below $\operatorname{supp}r\cup\operatorname{supp}b$, and above it
because $\int(r(x,t)-b(t)M(x))\,dt=0$; it vanishes for $x$ outside the $x$-projection of $\operatorname{supp}r$. The
$dy$-coefficient vanishes outside $\operatorname{supp}b$ in $y$, below $\operatorname{supp}M$ in $x$, and above it because
$\int M=0$. For the operator, every argument of $P_i$ has zero mass, so $dP_i(\cdot)$ returns it; the tree primitives
satisfy $dT_i=\eta_i-\eta_0$ (\S\ref{sec:native-toric}); hence
$d\mathcal P\omega=\omega-m(\omega)\eta_0=\omega$. All integrals are over fixed compact coordinate domains with smooth
integrands, which gives the support and smoothness statements.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The handle data of the diagonal quadric}\label{sec:shd-handle-data}

\begin{definition}[Finite presentation]\label{def:shd-presentation}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.quadricModelRawPresentation}
A finite presentation consists of coordinate maps with explicit inverses, compact inner and outer coordinate boxes,
partition weights $\zeta_i$ ($\zeta_i$ supported in outer box $i$, $\sum_i\zeta_i=1$ on the union of the inner boxes),
unit-mass bumps in the boxes and in the overlaps of parent and child, and a rooted tree on the boxes in which every
box $i\ne0$ has a parent $j<i$ whose overlap with box $i$ contains a coordinate box.
\end{definition}

\begin{construction}[Handle and normalisation data]\label{cons:shd-handle-data}

\begin{lemma}[Handle certificates]\label{lem:shd-handle-data}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.QuadricRawDual.actualDual_contMDiff,

\subsection{Normalisation and the budget}\label{sec:shd-datum}

\begin{lemma}[Normalised datum]\label{lem:shd-datum}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawNormalizationSpec,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.normalization_target}
Let $\gamma_*$ be the reserve of the comparison datum. The primitive $\Gamma_n$ of
Construction~\ref{cons:native-normalization} agrees with $\Gamma_{\rm phys}$ near $\partial\Sigma_\ell$, its base curvature
$d_\Sigma\Gamma_n(p)=\kappa_{\rm sm}(p)+H^*(p)\eta$ is positive for $p\in\Delta$, and on the annulus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shd-Gamma}
 \Gamma_n(p)=e_\Lambda\Gamma_{\rm ref}+f(s)H^*(p)\beta .
\end{equation}
The mass $H^*$ is smooth, nonnegative on $\Delta$, and $H^*\ge\Hb-\gamma_*/16$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
This is Proposition~\ref{prop:native-normalization}. Agreement near the boundary holds because the corrections
$\mathcal P(\cdot)$, $c_p\beta$ and $d_\Sigma(\zeta u_p)$ are supported in the correction core, in $\operatorname{supp}\beta$ and in the
annulus, all compact in $\operatorname{int}\Sigma_\ell$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-handle-data}).
\end{proof}

For given capacities $\rho_i<a_i$ the layers use
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shd-constants}
 \rho_i^\sharp=\frac{3a_i+\rho_i}{4},\qquad
 \varepsilon_i=\frac{a_i-\rho_i^\sharp}{4}.
\end{equation}
The collar bounds \eqref{eq:hdl-collar} and the definition \eqref{eq:hdl-ksm} of $H^*$ give, for $w\in V_\Delta$, by Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-layers}(ii),
\begin{multline}\label{eq:shd-budget}
 H^*\ge\Hb-\gamma_*/16,\\
 R^*(w)=K(p(q^{-1}w))-K(p(w))+H^*(p(w))-\sum_ih_i(p(w))\ge\gamma-\gamma_*/16>0 .
\end{multline}

\begin{lemma}[Nondegeneracy and the Gram cutoffs]\label{lem:shd-nbhd}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawFlowAtlasSpec.pathCutoff_spec}
Let $K=V_\Delta(E)$. For $j=0,1,2$ and $t\in[0,1]$ the forms $\Omega_{j,t}$ of \S\ref{sec:native-toric} are symplectic at every
point of $K$, hence on an open neighbourhood of $[0,1]\times K$ in $[0,1]\times E$. The cutoff $\chi_j$ of
\S\ref{sec:native-toric} equals one on a neighbourhood of $[0,1]\times K$, and $\chi_j(t,\cdot)$ has compact support contained in
the set where $\Omega_{j,t}$ is nondegenerate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All three paths are closed, being exact. By Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-top-power}(a), for a toric primitive $Q$ the top power of
$\omega_V+dQ$ is $n\,\omega_V^m\wedge d_\Sigma Q$; mixed terms $dp_j\wedge(\cdot)$ do not contribute.

\emph{Path $0$.} $d_\Sigma Q_{0,t}=(1-t)\kappa(p)+t(\kappa_{\rm sm}(p)+H^*\eta)$. For $p\in\Delta$, $\kappa(p)>0$
(\Mref{Definition}{def:mech-model}), $\kappa_{\rm sm}(p)$ is a convex combination of $\kappa(p)$ and $e_\Lambda\Lambda>0$, and
$H^*\eta\ge0$ since $H^*\ge0$ and $\eta=f'b\,ds\wedge dv\ge0$. So the base form is positive.

\emph{Path $1$.} Off $E|_{\mathcal A}$, $\Omega_{1,t}=\Omega_{0,1}$. On $E|_{\mathcal A}$, $\Omega_{1,t}=\mathcal D_t^*(\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(G_t\beta))$
(Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-suspension}(ii)), whose top power at $\mathcal D_t(x)$ is, by Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-top-power}(b),
$n(e_\Lambda a_\Lambda+b\,f'(s)H^*(p(q_t^{-1}w')))\,\omega_V^m\wedge ds\wedge dv$ with $w'$ the fibre point of $\mathcal D_t(x)$.
Since $q_\tau(V_\Delta)=V_\Delta$ for all $\tau$ (\Mref{Definition}{def:mech-datum}), $x\in K$ gives $w'\in V_\Delta$ and
$q_t^{-1}w'\in V_\Delta$, so $H^*\ge0$ there; $e_\Lambda a_\Lambda>0$, $b\ge0$, $f'\ge0$, and $\mathcal D_t$ is a diffeomorphism.

\emph{Path $2$.} On $E|_{\mathcal A}$, $\Omega_{2,t}=\mathcal D^*(\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(\hat G_t\beta))$ by \eqref{eq:hdl-final},
with top-power coefficient $e_\Lambda a_\Lambda+b\,\partial_s\hat G_t$ at $\mathcal D(x)$; here
$\partial_s\hat G_t=(1-t)f'H^*(p(q^{-1}w'))+t\,\partial_sG_{\rm f}\ge0$ on $\mathcal A\times V_\Delta$ by the previous case and
Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-layers}(iii). Off $E|_{\mathcal A}$, $\Omega_{2,t}=\Omega_{0,1}+t\,dJ\wedge\beta$ differs from
$\Omega_{0,1}$ by a mixed term and has the same top power.

Thus each $\Omega_{j,t}^n\ne0$ on the compact set $[0,1]\times K$; since nondegeneracy is an open condition and the forms
depend smoothly on $(t,x)$, it holds on an open neighbourhood. For the cutoff, at each point of $K$ some flow-atlas
bump $w_i$ equals one and the determinant of $\Omega_{j,t}$ in chart $i$ is nonzero, so $g_j>0$ on $[0,1]\times K$ and
$\mu_j>0$. The factor $S(4g_j/\mu_j-1)$ equals one where $g_j\ge\mu_j/2$, and $S(2(R+1-|w|^2))$ equals one where
$|w|^2\le R+\frac12$; both sets contain an open neighbourhood of $[0,1]\times K$. Conversely $\chi_j(t,z)\ne0$ forces
$g_j(t,z)>\mu_j/4>0$, so some $w_i(z)\ne0$ with nonzero determinant in chart $i$, and $|w|^2<R+1$; the set
$\{g_j(t,\cdot)\ge\mu_j/4,\ |w|^2\le R+1\}$ is closed, its base projection lies in the compact union of the supports of the $w_i$,
and it is therefore compact.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The three paths and the Moser maps}\label{sec:shd-first}

\begin{proposition}[First path]\label{prop:shd-first-path}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.relativePath_semantic,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.normalization_target}
The path $\Omega_{0,t}=\omega_V+d((1-t)\Gamma_{\rm phys}+t\Gamma_n)$ has the curvature, positivity and annular endpoint
stated in Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-first-path}. Its variation primitive $\alpha_1$ of \eqref{eq:hdl-alpha1}
is supported in the union of the correction core, $\operatorname{supp}\beta$ and the annulus.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The base curvature and positivity are Path $0$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-nbhd}; the endpoint is \eqref{eq:shd-Gamma}; the support
statement is the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-datum}.
\end{proof}


\begin{proposition}[Second path]\label{prop:shd-second-path}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.firstPath_semantic}
The path $\Omega_{1,t}=\omega_V+d(\Gamma_n+\eta_t)$, with $\eta_t$ the suspension primitive of Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-suspension},
is closed, equals $\Omega_{0,1}$ at $t=0$, and is symplectic on a neighbourhood of $V_\Delta(E)$. Over $\mathcal A$ it is the
pullback by $\mathcal D_t$ of $\omega_V+e_\Lambda\Lambda+d(G_t\beta)$, $G_t=f(s)H^*(p(q_t^{-1}w))$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The pullback identity is Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-suspension}(ii). The coefficient correction and the suspension term,
\[
 f(s)\bigl[H^*(p(q_t^{-1}q_{tf(s)}w))-H^*(p(w))\bigr]\qquad\text{and}\qquad(Q^t_s\circ q_{tf(s)})\,ds,
\]
vanish for $s\le0$ and $s\ge1$ (Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-suspension}(iii)), so extension by zero is smooth and $\eta_0=0$. Nondegeneracy is Path $1$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-nbhd}.
\end{proof}


\begin{proposition}[Third path]\label{prop:shd-layer-path}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.secondPath_semantic,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.native_surfaceRemainder_nonneg}
The path $\Omega_{2,t}=\omega_V+d(\Gamma_n+\eta_1+t\xi)$ is closed and symplectic on a neighbourhood of $V_\Delta(E)$. Over the
annulus it is the pullback by $\mathcal D$ of the interpolation from $G_1=fH^*\circ p\circ q^{-1}$ to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shd-Gf}
 G_{\rm f}=J\circ p+\sum_i\varsigma_i h_i\circ p+\varsigma_*R^*,\qquad J=K-H^*,
\end{equation}
whose $s$-derivative is nonnegative on $\mathcal A\times V_\Delta$. On ball layer $i$ only the toric coefficient
$A_i(p)+\varsigma_i(s)h_i(p)$ remains. Off the annulus the correction is the toric form $J(p)\beta$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The clocks have disjoint ordered transition intervals, and $h_i\ge0$, $R^*>0$ on $V_\Delta$ by \eqref{eq:shd-budget}; this gives
Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-layers}(iii). On both end collars of $\mathcal A$ the annular correction equals $J(p)\beta$, so
$\xi$ is smooth \eqref{eq:hdl-xi}. Nondegeneracy is Path $2$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-nbhd}.
\end{proof}


\begin{proposition}[The Moser maps]\label{prop:shd-moser}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawFlowAtlasSpec.inverse_pathFlow_eq,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation.fullMoserFlow_spec,
SymplecticPacking.HighDim.ToricPackingStages.RawSurfacePresentation.fullMoserFlow_diffeomorph}
For $j=0,1,2$ the cut field $Y_j$, defined by $\iota_{Y_{j,t}}\Omega_{j,t}=-\chi_j\,\partial_tQ_{j,t}$ on the nondegenerate locus
and by zero elsewhere, is smooth on $[0,1]\times E$ with compact spatial support. Its time-one map $\Phi_j$ is a diffeomorphism
of $E$ with inverse the backward flow, $\Phi_j^{\pm1}(K)=K$, $\Phi_j^{\pm1}(\operatorname{int}K)=\operatorname{int}K$, and
$\Phi_j^*\Omega_{j,1}=\Omega_{j,0}$ on $K$. These are the identities of Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-faces}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:shd-nbhd}, $\chi_j(t,\cdot)$ is supported in a compact subset of the nondegenerate locus, where the
defining equation has the smooth solution $-\Omega_{j,t}^{-1}(\chi_j\partial_tQ_{j,t})$ (Cramer's rule in each chart);
this solution vanishes near the boundary of the support, so the extension by zero is smooth. A smooth field with
compact spatial support on $[0,1]\times E$ has a complete flow, and its time-one map is a diffeomorphism whose inverse is the
backward flow.

On a neighbourhood $U$ of $[0,1]\times K$ we have $\chi_j=1$, so $Y_j$ is there the Moser field of the pair
$(\Omega_{j,t},\partial_tQ_{j,t})$, which satisfies $\partial_t\Omega_{j,t}=d\,\partial_tQ_{j,t}$. Lemma~\ref{lem:native-collar} shows
that every integral curve of $Y_j$ on a subinterval of $[0,1]$ which meets $K$ (resp.\ $\operatorname{int}K$) stays in $K$
(resp.\ $\operatorname{int}K$): near $\partial\Sigma_\ell$ the variation primitives vanish, so $Y_j=0$ there; and near each outer
face $\{\mu_\nu=\ell_\nu\}$ the face moment satisfies $\iota_{X_\nu}\Omega_{j,t}=-d\mu_\nu$ and $\partial_tQ_{j,t}(X_\nu)=0$, so
$d\mu_\nu(Y_{j,t})=0$ by Lemma~\ref{I:lem:pre-moser}(d). The same holds in reverse time. Lemma~\ref{I:lem:pre-moser}(e) applied to
$\mathcal K=K$ then gives $\Phi_j(K)=K$ and $\Phi_j^*\Omega_{j,1}=\Omega_{j,0}$ on $K$; applied to the backward flow it gives
$\Phi_j^{-1}(K)=K$, and the statement for $\operatorname{int}K$ follows in the same way, since every integral curve through a
point of $\operatorname{int}K$ stays in it.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Proof of the packing theorem in a model (\Mref{Theorem}{thm:mech-packing})}\label{sec:hdl-proof}

Let $a_i$, $P$, $(K,q)$ and $\gamma$ be as in \Mref{Theorem}{thm:mech-packing}, with \eqref{eq:hdl-collar}, and let $\rho_i<a_i$. Write $\C^n=\C\times\C^m\ni(z_{\rm b},z_{\rm f})$, $\varpi=\pi|z_{\rm b}|^2$ and $p=p(z_{\rm f})$, so $\omega_0=\omega_\C+\omega_V$ and $B(c)=\{\varpi+|p|\le c\}$. For $n=2$ the proof is completed in \PoneRef{rem:hdl-four} and \PoneRef{sec:shd-four}.

\emph{Parameters.} Put $\hat\rho_i=(3a_i+\rho_i)/4$ and $\varepsilon_i=(a_i-\hat\rho_i)/4$, so $B(\rho_i)\subset\Bo(\hat\rho_i)$; in \eqref{eq:hdl-caps} take $\rho_i^\sharp=\hat\rho_i$.

\emph{Forms and Moser map.} Construct $\Omega^{(2)}_\tau$, $\mathcal D$, $G_{\rm f}$ and $\Phi$ as above. Then $\Phi^*\Omega_{\rm final}=\Omega$ by Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-faces}, and $\Omega_{\rm final}=\mathcal D^*\Omega_{\rm pre}$ on $E|_{\mathcal A}$ by \eqref{eq:hdl-final}; $\mathcal D$ fixes base points, and its inverse $(s,v,w)\mapsto(s,v,q_{f(s)}^{-1}(w))$ preserves $\operatorname{int}V_\Delta$.

\emph{Layer maps.} For $(z_{\rm b},z_{\rm f})\in B^\circ(\hat\rho_i)$ let $(x,T)=\Psi_{\varepsilon_i}(z_{\rm b})$. Then $|p|<a_i$, so $z_{\rm f}\in\operatorname{int}V_\Delta$, and by Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-layers}(i),
\[
0<x<\varpi+\tfrac{\varepsilon_i}2<\hat\rho_i-|p|+\tfrac{a_i-\hat\rho_i}{8}<\tfrac{a_i+\hat\rho_i}{2}-|p|\le h_i(p).
\]
Hence
\[
 \mathcal E_i(z_{\rm b},z_{\rm f})=\Big(s_i\big(x,T,p\big),\ v(T),\ \mathrm{Sh}_{A_i,T}(z_{\rm f})\Big)\in\Lambda_i
\]
is defined, a composite of diffeomorphisms onto open sets. By \eqref{eq:hdl-layer-form}, Lemma~\ref{I:lem:hdl-shear} and Proposition~\ref{I:prop:hdl-strip}, $\mathcal E_i^*\Omega_{\rm pre}=(\Psi_{\varepsilon_i}\times\mathrm{id})^*(\omega_V+dx\wedge dT)=\omega_0$. Put
\[
 \psi_i=\Phi^{-1}\circ\mathcal D^{-1}\circ\mathcal E_i\colon B^\circ(\hat\rho_i)\to E .
\]
This is the map of Construction~\ref{cons:native-packing}; its factors $\Phi_j^{-1}$ are the inverse flows of $Q_0=(1-t)\Gamma_{\rm phys}+t\Gamma_n$, $Q_1=\Gamma_n+\eta_t$ and $Q_2=\Gamma_n+\eta_1+t\xi$.

\emph{Conclusions.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\psi_i^*\Omega=\mathcal E_i^*\Omega_{\rm pre}=\omega_0$, and $\psi_i$ is an injective immersion of an open set, hence a symplectic embedding of $\Bo(\hat\rho_i)\supset B(\rho_i)$.
\item $\psi_i$ takes values in $\operatorname{int}V_\Delta(E)$, since $\mathcal E_i$ maps there and $\mathcal D^{-1}$, $\Phi^{-1}$ preserve it (Proposition~\ref{prop:hdl-faces}).
\item The images are pairwise disjoint, because the intervals $(l_i,r_i)$ are disjoint and the common maps $\mathcal D^{-1}$, $\Phi^{-1}$ are injective.
\end{enumerate}
Every ingredient is a formula, a definite integral, the solution of an explicit ODE, or a unique root, so $\psi_i$ is explicit in the sense of \Mref{Definition}{def:intro-levels}; details are in \S\ref{sec:native-toric}. \qed

\subsection{Assembly}\label{sec:shd-proof}
\begin{theorem}[Packing in the model]\label{thm:shd-packing}
\lean{SymplecticPacking.HighDim.RawModelAgreement.native_packing_spec}
For the higher-dimensional model inputs, the maps $\psi_i$ of Construction~\ref{cons:native-packing}
satisfy \Mref{Theorem}{thm:mech-packing}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:shd-moser}, $\Phi=\Phi_2\circ\Phi_1\circ\Phi_0$ satisfies $\Phi^*\Omega_{\rm final}=\Omega$ on $K$ and
$\Phi^{-1}$ maps $\operatorname{int}K$ onto itself. The layer maps $E_i$ satisfy $E_i^*\Omega_{\rm pre}=\omega_0$ and have
pairwise disjoint images in $\operatorname{int}K$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:str-layer}), and $\mathcal D^{-1}$ pulls
$\Omega_{\rm final}$ back to $\Omega_{\rm pre}$ \eqref{eq:hdl-final}. The composite
$\psi_i=\Phi^{-1}\circ\mathcal D^{-1}\circ E_i$ is therefore a smooth symplectic embedding of $\Bo(\rho_i^\sharp)$, a
neighbourhood of $B(\rho_i)$, and the images are pairwise disjoint because $\mathcal D^{-1}$ and $\Phi^{-1}$ are single
injective maps (Proposition~\ref{prop:str-embeddings}).
\end{proof}
