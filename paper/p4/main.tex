\begin{abstract}
For every integer $k\ge10$ and every $\eps\in(0,1)$ we construct explicit $C^4$ symplectic embeddings
$f_0,\dots,f_{k-1}\colon\EB^{\circ}(r_+)\to\EB^{\circ}(R)$ in $\R^4$, where $r_+>1$ and $R=(k/(1-\eps))^{1/4}$, whose
images are pairwise disjoint, together with an explicit non-membership-certifying left inverse on the target ball.
Unlike the Hamiltonian flow construction of Paper~I, no ordinary differential equation is integrated anywhere: each
$f_i$ is a finite composition of elementary closed-form maps together with scalar roots of strictly monotone
one-variable equations (one Kepler equation in the inverse strip map and generating-function angle/radial equations in
two planar keyholes). The geometry adapts the degree-$9$ disc-bundle mechanism of Paper~I---linear comparison slope
$3\sqrt k-9$ and three marked fibres mapped to the line at infinity of $\CP^2$---into direct Darboux charts,
generating-function keyholes with explicit full-disc nesting and slit bounds, $C^4$ deck-equivariant toric twists, and a
piecewise closed-form transfer across the three edges of the moment simplex of $\CP^2$. All parameter inequalities hold
analytically for every $k\ge10$ and $\eps\in(0,1)$. A normalised Liouville primitive gives an explicit generator
formula for the radial--Alexander collar stage, together with abstract quantitative ODE trajectory certificates.
Sections~1--7 prove the exact-real algorithm implemented by \texttt{program/solution\_embedding.py}, and finite-precision
evaluation is checked numerically; lifting these direct maps to a standalone global Hamiltonian flow (distinct from the
$C^\infty_c$ flow already proved in Paper~I) is left to future work.
\end{abstract}
